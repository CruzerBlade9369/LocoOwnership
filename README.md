# Loco Ownership

Own more locomotives than the museum allows you to and customize them like your demonstrators!

Loco Ownership allows you to purchase DVRT locomotives, and own and operate them like demonstrators. Enjoy all the benefits of owning your own locomotives without being limited to one of each type!

- Purchase and own locomotives, separate from demonstrators.
- No license required, but you will need to have the license to the locomotive you want to own.
- Owned locomotives can only be serviced manually.
- Owned locomotives are paintable with any compatible paint.
- Owned locomotives don't despawn and won't contribute to your fee count.
- Sell locomotives when you don't want them anymore.

Visit the [wiki](https://github.com/CruzerBlade9369/LocoOwnership/wiki) for more information on all of the mod's features.

Some known incompatibilities with other mods:
- [Rolling Stock Ownership](https://github.com/fauxnik/dv-rolling-stock-ownership)
- [Multiplayer](https://github.com/AMacro/dv-multiplayer)
